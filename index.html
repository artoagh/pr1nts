Description

Pr1nts is a small 3D printing project. We create printed parts and other objects based on ideas and requests. Our goal is to turn your ideas into real prints and give you needed details and parts of something.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does printing take?

Printing time depends on the size, shape and complexity of the requested object.

What materials do you use?

We use PLA, PETG and TPU. The material depends on the requirements and purpose of your requested print.

Can I order my own design?

Yes! You can send your own 3D model or an idea of what you want to create.

Can I request a custom size?

Yes! Custom sizes are possible as long as the object fits within our maximum printing size of 17 cm³.

How can I place an order?

Send a message to 043 978744 with your idea, details and any reference pictures or 3D models.
